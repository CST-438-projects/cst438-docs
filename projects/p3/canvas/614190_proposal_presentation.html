Goal

Present the product your team will build, from the PRD, and the plan for building it. The class and the instructor should leave knowing who the product is for, what the MVP is, what the vertical slice is, and who owns what.

This is a group assignment, graded individually. The team turns in one submission. [TEAM] rubric rows are shared and [INDIVIDUAL] rows are graded per student. Requirements are in the What You Build and Design and Documentation sections of the Project 03 guide in cst438-docs.

Before class

  • docs/prd.md is committed to your team repo, using the PRD template. The commit date is checked on the Final Submission.
  • The slide deck is finished and every member knows which section they present.

The slides

The deck follows the PRD. It does not replace it.

  1. Title: product name, team number, members, and a link to the team repo.
  2. Users and problem: the primary user and the problem, as stated in the PRD.
  3. Success measures: the three measures, each with its target.
  4. Scope: the MVP stories (the account baseline plus your product stories) and the not-building list.
  5. Vertical slice: the one story you will build end to end first, with its Given/When/Then acceptance criteria.
  6. Technical plan:
    • An architecture sketch showing the API, web client, Android client, database and both OAuth providers.
    • A draft ERD: users plus at least two other resource types and their relationships.
    • The API endpoints the vertical slice needs.
    • Where each piece will be hosted.
  7. Ownership: the slice of work each member owns, and the Sprint 1 merge captain and deputy.
  8. Risks and open questions: what you do not know yet and how you will find out.

In class

  • The timebox is set in class.
  • Every member presents at least one section.
  • Questions follow the presentation. Any member may be asked to answer.

Turn in (one per team)

A PDF of the slides. The title slide links to the team repo and to docs/prd.md.

After the presentation, change the PRD only through pull requests.
