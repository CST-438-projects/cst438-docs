Goal

A short stand-up with the instructor. Every member should be able to say what they are working on right now and how it fits their role on the team. One person speaking for everyone does not count.

This is a group assignment, graded individually. There is nothing to submit; it is graded during the meeting. Keep it brief: about 15 minutes for the whole team.

Each member answers

  1. What did you work on since the last meeting?
  2. What are you working on right now? Point to the issue on the board. It is assigned to you.
  3. Are you stuck anywhere? If so, what is your plan?

Be ready to explain the vertical slice you own and to name this sprint's merge captain and deputy.

Meeting 4: demo

Bring the app up on production, deployed from a tagged release. Each member shows a feature they built.

Requirements are in the Agile Process and the Merge Captain section of the Project 03 guide in cst438-docs. Teams and repos are on the Project 03 Groups, Repos, Etc. page. Graded with the P3 Agile Meeting 4/4 rubric.
