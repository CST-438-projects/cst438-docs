Goal

Confirm that the team exists on GitHub, that everyone can reach the repo, and that the team has picked a product it can describe. The instructor meets each team in class for about 10 to 15 minutes.

This is a group assignment, graded individually. [TEAM] rubric rows are shared and [INDIVIDUAL] rows are graded per student. Requirements are in the Teams, Repos, and Hosting and What You Build sections of the Project 03 guide in cst438-docs.

Before the meeting

  • One teammate creates a free GitHub organization for the team and invites everyone else as owners.
  • The team repo is public and inside the team organization, never in CST-438-projects. A monorepo (api/, app/, docs/, compose.yml) is recommended.
  • Every member has accepted the organization invitation.
  • The team's row on the Project 03 Groups, Repos, Etc. sheet has the app title and the GitHub organization.

Be ready to answer

  • Who is the one primary user, and what problem do they have?
  • What resource types besides users does the product have, and how are they related?
  • What is the MVP: the account baseline plus which product stories?
  • Which story is the candidate vertical slice?
  • What will the team deliberately not build?
  • What will each member own?
  • Who is the Sprint 1 merge captain, and who is the deputy?

The answers become docs/prd.md, which is committed before the proposal presentation.

Turn in

Nothing. The instructor records the results during the meeting.
