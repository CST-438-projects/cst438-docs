Goal

Rehearse the final presentation on production while there is still time to fix what goes wrong, and show that the team can operate its own system under a small surprise.

This is a group assignment, graded individually. The team turns in one submission. [TEAM] rubric rows are shared and [INDIVIDUAL] rows are graded per student. Requirements are in the Dry Run, Scenario Cards, and Final Presentation section of the Project 03 guide in cst438-docs.

Before class

  • Production is up at your production URL, deployed from a tagged release.
  • docs/demo.md and docs/runbook.md are committed and current.
  • Every member can open the production app and the repo on their own laptop.
  • Rehearse the whole scenario-card deck in the guide. You will not know which card you draw.

In class

  1. Demo run-through. Run the demo against the production URL, following docs/demo.md, as you would at the final presentation. Note every place it stalls, breaks or runs long.
  2. Scenario card. Draw one card from the deck and perform it live on production, using your runbook. If the runbook does not cover a step, say so and keep going.
  3. PR walkthroughs. Each member is handed one of their own merged PRs and explains what it does, why it was built that way and which test covers it.

The scenario card and the PR walkthroughs are scored on the Final Submission rubric. This assignment grades the rehearsal itself and what the team does with what it learned.

After the dry run

File every problem found (demo, scenario card, runbook gaps, walkthrough questions nobody could answer) as a GitHub issue labeled dry-run, with an owner and a milestone. Update docs/runbook.md wherever the scenario card exposed a gap.

Turn in (one per team)

One PDF containing:

  • Your team number and the members present.
  • The production URL and the release tag that was running.
  • The card you drew, what you did and what happened.
  • A link to each dry-run issue, with its owner.
  • A link to the runbook commit or PR that closed the gaps, if the card exposed any.
