Goal

Show the work you merged this sprint and the review process behind it. Requirements are in the Agile Process and the Merge Captain section of the Project 03 guide in cst438-docs.

This is a SOLO assignment. Everyone submits their own PDF.

Deadlines

  • 2:00 AM Thursday: your PR is open and this report is submitted. Open it later and you lose the review-window points.
  • 10:00 AM Thursday: PRs are reviewed and merged. If a teammate opened a PR by 2:00 AM and requested your review, review it by 10:00 AM or you lose the reviewer-duty points.
  • Your team's own deadline in docs/team_agreement.md comes earlier. Missing it does not cost points here; it comes up at the agile meeting and in teammate reviews.
  • Only this sprint's captain or deputy merges. A PR merged before its approvals costs the author the approvals points and the captain the merge-captain points.

What you submit

One PDF with working links that match GitHub:

  • Link to your PR, with your own real code, merged to main this sprint.
  • Issue(s) addressed: the PR says Closes #N for a story on this sprint's milestone. Give the PRD story ID.
  • Next issue: a link to it. It must be assigned to you.
  • Reviewers: who approved it (2 teammates who are not the author, 1 on a team of 3), and the PRs you reviewed.
  • Branch name. Do not work on main.
  • Tests: what you tested, a link to the passing CI run on the PR, and the coverage number.
  • This sprint's captain and deputy, matching docs/captains.md.
  • Team agreement: a permalink to docs/team_agreement.md as it stood this sprint (open the file on GitHub and press y).
  • If you were captain this sprint, a merge log: every PR that merged (and who merged it), every PR that waited and why, and a link to the sprint's tagged release.

Graded with the P3 Iteration 2/4 PR Report rubric.
