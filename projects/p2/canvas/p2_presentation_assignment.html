This is a group project presentation for your REST API project. You will present your work live to the class in approximately 6 minutes. Your instructor will grade you in real time as you present.

This is a lightweight assignment but it carries a serious requirement: you must speak to your own contributions. If you have no contributions to the project, or if you do not discuss them during your presentation, you will receive a 0 for this assignment, regardless of how your group performs.

Where the rest is graded. Your code, deployment and documentation are graded on the Project 02 GROUP: Final Submission (code and deployment) assignment. This assignment grades the presentation itself: that you were ready, that you used your time well, and that you communicated your own work clearly.

What to Prepare

Each group must create a slide deck that includes all of the following:

  • Your name displayed prominently
  • Your photo
  • A link to your project (repository, live deployment, or both)

During your 6-minute presentation, cover the following:

  1. What you personally worked on
    1.  be specific about your contributions to the codebase
  2. What is working
    1. show a recorded demo or walkthrough of your API in action
  3. What you are proud of
    1. highlight something you built or solved that you found meaningful or challenging
  4. How it works
    1. briefly explain the technical approach behind one or more features you built

Deliverable

Submit your slide deck link via this Canvas assignment before your presentation. Your slides must be accessible to CSUMB.

There is nothing else to submit. The presentation itself is the deliverable.

The Zero-Contribution Policy

If you did not contribute to the project, or if you do not discuss your contributions during the presentation, your score for this assignment is 0; no exceptions.

This policy exists because the presentation is your opportunity to demonstrate ownership of your work. Showing up without contributions, or remaining silent while your group presents, does not satisfy the requirements of this assignment. If you have concerns about your group situation, please speak with your instructor before presentation day.

Tips for a Strong Presentation

  • Practice your presentation before presentation day 
    • I will ask you to show the live server
  • Know what you built well enough to answer a follow-up question about it
  • Six minutes goes quickly
    • be concise and focused
  • You do not need to present every feature of the project, just your piece of it
